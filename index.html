Golden Way Holding’s Sustainable Refining

Innovating for a Cleaner Energy Future

At Golden Way Holding, we recognize that responsible energy production is critical for our planet’s future. Our refineries are pioneering low-carbon operations, circular economy principles, and next-generation clean fuel technologies to reduce environmental impact while meeting global energy demands.

Reducing Carbon Footprint

  • Carbon Capture & Utilization (CCU): Implementing systems to capture CO₂ emissions for industrial reuse
  • Flare Gas Recovery: Converting waste gases into usable energy
  • Energy Optimization: AI-driven process efficiency to reduce fuel consumption by up to 15%

Renewable Energy Integration: Solar/wind power for auxiliary refinery operations

Circular Economy in Refining

  • Waste-to-Fuel Conversion: Reprocessing sludge/residues into usable hydrocarbons
  • Plastic Pyrolysis Oil: Transforming non-recyclable plastics into refinery feedstocks
  • Water Recycling: >90% process water reused through advanced treatment
  • Spent Catalyst Rejuvenation: Extending lifecycle of refining catalysts

Cleaner Fuel Production

  • Ultra-Low Sulfur Fuels: <10ppm sulfur diesel/gasoline meeting Euro 6/BS VI standards
  • Biofuel Blending: 20% ethanol-gasoline & biodiesel-diesel blends
  • Hydrogen-Rich Refining: Transitioning to hydrogen-based cracking processes
  • E-Fuels R&D: Exploring synthetic fuels from green hydrogen + captured carbon

Certifications & Partnerships

  •  ISO 14001 & 50001 Certified Environmental/Energy Management.
  •  Member of OGMP 2.0 (UN-led methane reduction initiative)
  •  World Bank ZERO Routine Flaring Commitment
  •  Collaborations with IEA Clean Energy Transitions Program
